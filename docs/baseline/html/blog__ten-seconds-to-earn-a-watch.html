Ten seconds to earn a watch: notes on vertical ad films

Jul 24, 2026/3 min read

A glass smash in the first second, a chase to keep it going, and the bottle in frame the whole time. What we learned making ads people finish.

  1. 01

    The first second is the brief

    On a phone, nobody owes an ad their attention. For The Getaway we opened on a car window breaking, because a sound and a movement like that earns the next few seconds on its own.

  2. 02

    Shoot vertical from the start

    Cropping a wide film for Reels always shows. We framed tight inside the car and shot the chase handheld, so it feels caught on the spot rather than staged.

  3. 03

    Keep the product in every frame

    The bottle is the reason for the chase, so it never leaves the shot. If someone swipes away at second four, they have still seen what we were selling.
