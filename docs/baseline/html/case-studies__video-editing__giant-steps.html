Giant Steps

“Bigger than the city.”

Client
Giant Steps
Service
VFX Edit
Category
Streetwear / CGI

The challenge

A streetwear drop wanted a piece of content that looked impossible and still felt real.

  1. 01

    The Edit

    We composited the model into live New York street footage, matching the light, lens and camera shake so the scale holds up.

  2. 02

    Selling The Shot

    A cut to a view from inside a car gives the audience a second angle, which makes the giant feel physically there.

OUTCOME

The Result

A ten second clip made to be shared, with the outfit readable in every frame.
